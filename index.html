DOM VIRUS / CO-OP CARTRIDGE · V0.2.0

DOM Crossfire.
Pick a ride.

Pick the plane or the tank. The other one fights beside you as your AI partner. The bad guys of four DOM VIRUS games come at you in waves: bugs, invaders, tank squads and flies. Then all four at once.

01 HOW CROSSFIRE PLAYS

Two rides.
One fight.

  1. 01

    PICK YOUR RIDE

    Plane or tank

    Pick on the start screen; it remembers. The one you don't pick is your partner.

  2. 02

    YOUR PARTNER

    An AI, or your squad

    Solo, an AI flies or drives the other ride and goes after what it's good at. Or play as a squad of 2 to 4, each in the plane or the tank.

  3. 03

    FOUR SECTIONS

    Four waves an army

    Swarm Protocol, Null Invaders, Sherman Bytes, then Byte Runner: four waves each, each wave tougher, a mini-boss on the fourth. Then the finale: all four.

  4. 04

    DROPS

    Shared reinforcements

    You start with four. Every section you clear adds one. Go down with none left and it's INSERT COIN.

  5. 05

    THE BLINKING GRID

    Slot-machine squares

    Rapid fire, triple shot, shields, health, ammo, airstrikes. And mines: the plane flies over them, the tank doesn't.

  6. 06

    AIRSTRIKES

    Tap the spot

    A bomber crosses the board and the bombs walk over the target: walls, factories, swarms.

  7. 07

    STREAKS AND TIERS

    Stronger mid-fight

    Streaks pay out long shots, strikes and a carpet bomb. The plane's guns climb Null Invaders' tiers to Overdrive.

  8. 08

    SUPPLY CRATES

    Parachuted in

    A medkit, ammo, an airstrike or the jackpot, every 20 seconds or so. Race your partner for it.

02 THE FOUR ARMIES

Their bad guys.
Your problem.

Each wave brings the enemies of one DOM VIRUS game, and they fight the way they do at home.

SECTION 1 · FROM SWARM PROTOCOL ↗

The swarm

Bugs walk in from the edges and eat the board's letters, one at a time, into binary. Six letters and they grow; twelve and they hunt you.

PLANE · PICK THEM OFFTANK · SQUASH THE SMALL ONES

SECTION 2 · FROM NULL INVADERS ↗

The button factories

Every button is a factory. Drones, crabs and squids keep coming out, swoop at you and drop bolts until the button breaks.

PLANE · BREAK THE FACTORIESTANK · COVER THE PLANE

SECTION 3 · FROM SHERMAN BYTES ↗

The fortified bases

Tank squads hold button bases: four walls round a core with a gun. Breach a wall, wreck the button, and the base is yours. Park in it to repair.

TANK · BREACH THE WALLSPLANE · CLEAR THE SKY

SECTION 4 · FROM BYTE RUNNER ↗

The frog and its flies

Byte Runner, flipped: every one of them is a bad guy now, the frog too. The pellets on the blocks are their food, and each one they eat makes them tougher. The frog grows with every pellet, and a big frog's tongue eats a plane or a tank whole: when it marks your spot, move.

PLANE · KILL THEM BEFORE THEY EATTANK · WATCH THE TONGUE

FINALE · CROSSFIRE

All four at once

A factory, a base, the frog and its flies, and a swarm, on one board. Stick together: each ride covers what the other can't.

04 CONTROLS

Same keys as Blitz.

WASD drive or fly · mouse aim · click / space fire · E long shot (tank) or salvo (plane) · F airstrike, then click the spot · Q auto-aim · T aim lock · Esc pause.

Phones: the stick bottom left drives; FIRE, AUTO, LOCK, the special and STRIKE sit under your right thumb, and the grab bar moves them.

DROP-IN · ANY WEBSITE

Put Crossfire on your page.

One line. A PLAY CROSSFIRE key appears; press it and your page becomes the arena: shells knock your headings around, treads flatten them, and the waves keep coming. Escape gives the page back exactly as it was.
