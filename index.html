DOM Crossfire

The virus scrambled the cartridges. One arena, one score. Every 30 seconds the console glitches and the rules change channel: same stick, new game. Survive all five.

  1. CH 1 Null Invaders: the fleet descends.
  2. CH 2 Byte Runner: no gun. Eat the bytes, dodge the flies.
  3. CH 3 Sherman Bytes: the turret aims itself.
  4. CH 4 Swarm Protocol: the swarm floods in.
  5. CH 5 DOM Battlefield: the grid locks your moves.

The page is the board

This is an ordinary page. Its words, headings and buttons are what the game plays on; when the round ends, or you press Escape, every letter goes back where it was.

Controls

  • Arrows / WASD / touch stick — move
  • Space / hold the pointer — fire
  • P — pause · Esc — give the page back
